Artist

Friedrich Pittelkow

Friedrich Pittelkow works where listening becomes a way of looking. His practice moves through sound, code, installation, and research into the fragile systems that usually stay below the threshold of attention.

The work asks how places, materials, and networks can be heard before they are explained.

01

Signal

The work is interested in interference: the productive noise between bodies and devices, rooms and atmospheres, technical systems and lived perception. It treats sound less as decoration than as evidence.

02

Method

Projects often begin with a small question: what does a place emit, what does a tool hide, what happens when data is made physical? From there, sketches become instruments, interfaces, field notes, or temporary environments.

03

Attention

monointerferenz gathers references, artifacts, and tools around that practice. It is part notebook and part index: a place for work that listens back.

For contact, collaborations, or quiet transmissions: post@monointerferenz.de

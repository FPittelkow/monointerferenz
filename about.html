Current operator: friedrich w. a. pittelkow

monointerferenz is a media art practice working through experimental sound, electronics, coding, and mixed media.

The work combines generative systems, signal processing, software, hardware, and physical computation to investigate emergent behavior across technical, biological, and physical environments.

01

Systems

Signals, materials, organisms, and computational structures are approached as interconnected environments rather than isolated mediums.

02

Process

Projects develop through experimentation, observation, iteration, and interaction.

03

Interference

Interference is approached as both phenomenon and method: overlaps between signal and noise, order and instability, control and disruption.

04

Emergence

Artistic experimentation functions as a speculative and open-ended form of research influenced by biology, physics, cybernetics, and media theory. The practice focuses on behaviors and structures that emerge through interaction between systems and exceed the properties of their individual components.

05

Environment

Installations, sound works, generative visuals, instruments, and mixed media environments are developed as responsive structures that unfold over time.
